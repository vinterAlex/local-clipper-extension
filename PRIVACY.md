# Privacy Policy for Local Clipper

Local Clipper is committed to protecting your privacy.

- **Data Collection:** Local Clipper collects text snippets and web page content that you explicitly choose to save.
- **Data Storage:** All saved clips, saved pages, snapshots, and user preferences are stored **100% locally on your device**. Snippets and settings use Chrome's local storage; full-page article bodies and snapshots use the browser's local IndexedDB database. No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.
- **Offline images:** When saving a page, images are embedded only from content your browser has already loaded (same-origin, blob, or data URLs). The extension does not fetch remote images or any other resources while saving. Remote images that could not be embedded are referenced by URL and only load if you open the saved page while online (with `no-referrer` applied).
- **Network Activity:** The **only** network request the extension makes is validating a Pro license key against Lemon Squeezy when you click "Activate License". There is no analytics, telemetry, or background syncing.
- **Data Sharing:** We do not sell, transfer, or share your data with anyone.
- **Contact:** If you have questions, reach out via our GitHub repository issues page.
