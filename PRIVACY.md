# Privacy Policy for Local Clipper

Local Clipper is committed to protecting your privacy. 

- **Data Collection:** Local Clipper collects text snippets and web content that you explicitly choose to clip. 
- **Data Storage:** All clipped data and user preferences are stored **100% locally on your device** using Chrome's local storage. No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.
- **Data Sharing:** We do not sell, transfer, or share your data with anyone.
- **Contact:** If you have questions, reach out via our GitHub repository issues page.
